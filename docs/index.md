# 📘 Security Risk Management - DisasterReady

This repository contains my study notes, resources, and exercises from the **Security Risk Management** course by [DisasterReady](https://www.disasterready.org/).

## Structure
- **cert/** → Certificate of completion
- **docs/** → Core documents (syllabus, roadmap, glossary, references)
- **notes/** → Module-wise notes
- **labs/** → Exercises and hands-on practice
- **extras/** → Case studies, resources, timeline
- **screenshots/** → Visuals of course overview & syllabus

## Learning Objectives
- Understand key principles of risk management
- Identify and assess different types of risks
- Apply structured approaches to risk analysis
- Develop mitigation and treatment plans
- Strengthen crisis management and incident response
- Align practices with governance, compliance, and standards
