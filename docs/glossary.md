# 📖 Glossary - Security Risk Management

- **Risk** → The effect of uncertainty on objectives
- **Threat** → A potential cause of an unwanted incident
- **Vulnerability** → Weakness that can be exploited by threats
- **Impact** → Consequences if a risk materializes
- **Likelihood** → Probability of a risk occurring
- **Risk Appetite** → The amount of risk an organization is willing to accept
- **Risk Treatment** → Measures to modify risk
- **Residual Risk** → Remaining risk after treatment
- **Business Continuity** → Ability to continue essential functions during/after disruption
- **Crisis Management** → Coordinated actions to manage severe disruptive events
- **Incident Response** → Organized approach to handling and mitigating security incidents
- **Governance** → Structures and processes to ensure accountability and oversight
