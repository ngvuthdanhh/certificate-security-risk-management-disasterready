# 📂 Case Studies - Security Risk Management

## Case Study 1: WannaCry Ransomware (2017)
- **Risk**: Unpatched systems exploited by EternalBlue
- **Impact**: 200,000+ computers affected globally, NHS hospitals disrupted
- **Lessons**:
  - Patch management is critical
  - Incident response must be swift
  - Backup and recovery strategies are essential

---

## Case Study 2: Maersk Cyber Attack (2017)
- **Risk**: NotPetya malware spread across the network
- **Impact**: Shipping and logistics halted worldwide, ~$300M loss
- **Lessons**:
  - Segmentation of networks can reduce impact
  - Business continuity planning avoids total shutdown

---

## Case Study 3: Haiti Earthquake (2010)
- **Risk**: Natural disaster disrupting humanitarian operations
- **Impact**: Communication breakdown, aid delivery delayed
- **Lessons**:
  - Crisis management and contingency planning are vital
  - Collaboration with local partners improves resilience
