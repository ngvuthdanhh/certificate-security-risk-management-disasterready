# 🧩 Risk Assessment Exercises

## Exercise 1: Risk Identification
- Identify 5 potential risks for an NGO delivering aid in a conflict zone  
- Classify them into categories: operational, financial, reputational, security

## Exercise 2: Risk Matrix
- Rate likelihood (Low / Medium / High) and impact (Low / Medium / High)  
- Place risks in a **3x3 matrix**  

Example:

| Risk                     | Likelihood | Impact | Priority |
|---------------------------|------------|--------|----------|
| Cyber-attack on systems  | High       | High   | Critical |
| Supply chain disruption  | Medium     | High   | High     |
| Employee turnover        | Low        | Medium | Moderate |

## Exercise 3: Scenario Analysis
- An earthquake hits the organization’s HQ  
- Identify immediate risks, secondary risks, and long-term risks
