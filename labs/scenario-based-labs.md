# 🎯 Scenario-Based Labs

## Scenario 1: Data Breach
- Situation: Sensitive donor information leaked
- Tasks:
  1. Identify root causes (e.g., weak passwords, no MFA)
  2. Assess impacts (financial, reputational, legal)
  3. Suggest treatments (encryption, staff training, access controls)

## Scenario 2: Natural Disaster
- Situation: Flood damages main office
- Tasks:
  1. List affected operations
  2. Propose continuity measures (cloud backups, remote work plan)
  3. Define recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO)

## Scenario 3: Insider Threat
- Situation: Disgruntled employee attempts sabotage
- Tasks:
  1. Identify vulnerabilities
  2. Suggest monitoring & HR collaboration
  3. Design incident response steps
