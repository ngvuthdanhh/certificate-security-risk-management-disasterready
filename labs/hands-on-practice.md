# 🛠️ Hands-on Practice

## Task 1: Build a Risk Register
- Create a table with: Risk, Category, Likelihood, Impact, Owner, Treatment Plan  

Example:

| Risk                  | Category   | Likelihood | Impact | Owner  | Treatment     |
|------------------------|------------|------------|--------|--------|---------------|
| Ransomware attack     | Security   | High       | High   | IT Sec | Backup + EDR  |
| Funding shortfall     | Financial  | Medium     | High   | CFO    | Diversify donors |

---

## Task 2: Draft a Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
- Identify critical business functions  
- Define RTO & RPO  
- Map backup strategies  

---

## Task 3: Incident Response Tabletop Exercise
- Scenario: Phishing campaign targets employees  
- Steps:
  1. Detect suspicious emails  
  2. Contain (block sender, isolate systems)  
  3. Eradicate (remove malware, patch systems)  
  4. Recover (restore backups, reset passwords)  
  5. Lessons learned (improve awareness training)
